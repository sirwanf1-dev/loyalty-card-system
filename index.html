Simple for customers. Useful for local businesses.

Customer account

Use your phone number to log in or create your customer account. No password needed.

For security, the live service should verify your number with a one-time SMS code. No password is required.

Business account

Sign in or create an account for your shop.

The live dashboard will include shop setup, QR generation, stamp rules and rewards. Secure registration is not connected yet.

Scan a shop QR code

Log in first, then scan the QR code displayed inside a participating shop.

▦
QR check-in

The live scanner will open here when customer sign-in is connected.

A QR scan alone will not add a stamp. The shop must verify the visit, and the server will enforce the 24-hour rule.
